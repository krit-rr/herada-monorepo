Herada Featured in the Pittsburgh Post-Gazette

November 15, 2025

Herada was recently featured in the Pittsburgh Post-Gazette as one of six startups to watch following completion of the Anvil Founder Coaching Program.

If you have a subscription, you can read the article here.

Being featured was meaningful not simply as recognition, but as validation that the challenges we're addressing—preventable friction in healthcare driven by administrative complexity and delayed visibility—resonate beyond startup circles.

The article highlights how early-stage teams across Pittsburgh are approaching real problems with focus and restraint. We're proud to be included among founders who are building thoughtfully and grounded in real-world constraints.

We're thankful to the Anvil team, mentors, and fellow founders whose support helped make this moment possible, and we're excited to build on this momentum as Herada enters its next phase.
