Reaching an Early Funding Milestone

October 25, 2025

We're excited to share that Herada has raised $100,000 in early funding.

This milestone reflects strong early belief in the problem we're tackling: preventable friction in healthcare caused by administrative complexity, reactive workflows, and limited early visibility for patients and care teams.

The support we've received enables us to continue focused product development, deeper customer discovery, and early pilot exploration—while staying grounded in clinical and operational realities.

This capital will be used to:

  • Advance product design and engineering
  • Continue validation with patients and care teams
  • Build responsibly, with real-world constraints at the center

We're deeply grateful to the early supporters and advisors who have helped shape Herada's direction so far.
