What Herada has built and is building. Each one teaches something nobody else does: to women, what to expect and what they’re owed; to clinicians, how it presents in women.

    Women’s Health Bible

    A free guide to the medical and insurance basics women need to navigate the healthcare system. Each life stage now opens with what’s covered, and the source for it.

  • Reviewing

    For women and cliniciansEvidence of what should have been considered before a perimenopause or anxiety label settled: ten checks, each with its guideline source.

    Rule-Out Checklist

    One printable page: routine checks and symptom-triggered ones, with why each matters and where the guidance comes from. Check it against your own history in the Ledger. Pending clinical review.

  • Polishing

    For womenEvidence of what was asked, tested and told across visits, kept by the patient so the next clinician can see it.

    The Ledger

    Log symptoms, visits, tests and treatments once. The Ledger keeps the record and builds the Visit Kit from it: your timeline, one-pager, what’s been tested and worth asking, and your questions for today. Local-only, encrypted backup. Pending clinical review.

  • Reviewing

    For cliniciansEvidence of how long women wait for the right diagnosis, how often the first label is wrong, and which numbers hold up.

    The Diagnostic Detour

    For midlife conditions: how long women wait for a diagnosis, how often the first label is wrong, how that compares to men, and whether trials enroll women at the rate conditions affect them. Every figure graded by source strength.

  • Almost There

    Pop Quiz

    Guess which circulating women’s-health stats hold up, and find your exam-room archetype. Every reveal is sourced; nothing you answer is stored.
