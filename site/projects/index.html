Projects

What Herada has built and is building. Each one is a way of learning what women need from the healthcare system.

  • Reviewing

    Women's Health Bible

    A free guide to the medical and insurance basics women need to navigate the healthcare system.

  • Polishing

    The Ledger

    Log symptoms, visits, tests and treatments once — the Ledger keeps the record and builds the Visit Kit from it: your timeline, one-pager, what's been tested and worth asking, and your questions for today. Local-only, encrypted backup. Pending clinical review.

  • Almost There

    Pop Quiz

    Guess which circulating women's-health stats hold up, and find your exam-room archetype. Every reveal is sourced; nothing you answer is stored.

  • Reviewing

    The Diagnostic Detour

    For midlife conditions: how long women wait for a diagnosis, how often the first label is wrong, how that compares to men — and whether trials enroll women at the rate conditions affect them. Every figure graded by source strength.

  • Prototype

    Coverage Monitor

    A demo that flags upcoming clinic visits at risk of insurance denials, delays and surprise bills. Synthetic data only.

  • Hackathon, 2023

    Her Health Advocate

    Where Herada started: a web app with an AI health assistant, a daily diary and a way to find other women nearby. Built with Aditi, Arushi and Sharika at SheInnovates 2023.
