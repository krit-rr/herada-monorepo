Privacy

What this site does with your information, which is almost nothing, written plainly.

On this site

  • These are static pages served by GitHub Pages. There are no accounts and no server of ours that stores anything.
  • We set no cookies and run no analytics, no advertising pixels and no session recording. There is no third-party script on any page.
  • The fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google's servers see that request, as they would for any site using them. Nothing else leaves the page.

In the tools

  • The Ledger, the Rule-Out Checklist and the quizzes run entirely in your browser. The Ledger saves to your browser's local storage on your device; the checklist and the quizzes save nothing at all. "Copy to notes" writes to your clipboard and nowhere else.
  • Printouts, images, copied text and backup files are generated on your device. A passphrase-protected backup is encrypted in the browser before it is saved.
  • "Clear all" in the Ledger removes everything it stored. Clearing your browser's site data does the same.
  • Nothing you check, enter or answer is sent to us, and we have no way to see it.

Email

If you write to us, your message stays in our inbox and is used only to reply. We do not add you to a list without asking first.

The Learning Library and the Registry

The sign-up on the Learn page is a set of email links that open your own mail app. There is no form on this site, and opening the page collects nothing. When it opens, the Misrouting Registry will run on a separate, consented form service, not on this site, with its terms shown before the first question. Results are published in aggregate and never sold.

If something goes wrong

Because nothing is stored on a server of ours, the realistic risks are a tampered deployment or a mistake in a tool update. Our response plan: fix it, say so on this site, and notify anyone affected within the deadlines the FTC's Health Breach Notification Rule sets.
