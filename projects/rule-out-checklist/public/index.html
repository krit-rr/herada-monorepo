Before “it’s probably perimenopause” settles.

Fatigue, brain fog, joint pain and palpitations in midlife are often put down to perimenopause or anxiety — sometimes rightly. This page shows which conditions share your symptoms, and what’s worth weighing before any label becomes the whole answer.

Why this exists: NICE guidance (NG23) has clinicians diagnose perimenopause in women over 45 from symptoms alone — no labs required. That is reasonable practice, and it also means the label can settle before the look-alikes are weighed.

Why this page exists

Fatigue, brain fog, joint pain and palpitations are not vague symptoms — they are shared symptoms, each appearing in at least four of the ten conditions this page maps, and fatigue in all ten. That is exactly why pattern-matching on any single symptom misroutes people, and why guidance that rightly allows a symptoms-only diagnosis can let the look-alikes go unweighed. The fix isn’t more testing for everyone; it’s making the consider-list explicit, so the explanation that remains has actually earned it.
