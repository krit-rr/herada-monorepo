Tell the story once.

Every new clinician starts from zero, and the first fifteen minutes go to history-taking. The Visit Kit builds three pages that do it for you: your timeline, what’s already been tested, and the three questions you want answered today.

Page 1 · The timeline

What happened, and when

From remembered history, not daily logging — roughly when things started is plenty. Symptoms become bars (thicker = more severe, raspberry = ongoing); life events become markers.

    Add a symptom and the timeline draws itself here.

    Reading it: each line is a symptom — the thicker the line, the more severe. A raspberry line is still ongoing. Diamonds mark life events.
