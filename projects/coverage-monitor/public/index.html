Coverage Monitor

See coverage problems before the visit. Coverage Monitor checks a clinic's upcoming schedule against each health plan's rules and lists the visits likely to end in a denial, a delay or a surprise bill, with what to do and by when.

How It Works

Connect

Reads the schedule, the authorizations on file and each plan's coverage rules. In this demo all three are sample data built into the page.

Anticipate

Runs every upcoming visit through a set of rules and works out which problems will land before the visit date.

Act

Gives front-office staff one list, ordered by urgency, with the next step and the last day it can still work.

The Rules Behind the Flags

  • DelayPrior authorization is required and has not been requested, or was requested too late for the plan's usual turnaround.
  • DenialAn approved authorization expires before the visit date.
  • DenialThe plan covers the service once in a set period and the last one was too recent.
  • DenialThe plan requires a referral or a documented first-line treatment, and neither is on file.
  • DenialCoverage showed as inactive at the last eligibility check.
  • SurpriseEligibility has not been checked in over 30 days, or the clinician is out of network for the patient's plan.
