The Visit Kit · Page 3 · Tested & worth asking

What’s been checked + what hasn’t

The consider-list for the midlife look-alikes, filled from your logged tests: a check is ticked because a matching test is in your log. The unticked boxes are their own message; “log it” adds one. Each item’s reasoning sits under “why · source.”

This is not an order sheet. Broad lab panels are low-yield: testing everyone for everything mostly finds noise, which is why family-medicine guidance keeps the first panel small. The ask is “was this considered?”, and “not needed, because…” is a perfectly good answer. Which of these fit your picture is your clinician’s call.

Who sees what

  • Cycles and hormones → OB-GYN or a menopause specialist
  • Thyroid, metabolic → endocrinology
  • Joints, immune, multi-system → rheumatology
  • Heart, palpitations → cardiology
  • Unrefreshing sleep → sleep medicine
  • Lifelong focus trouble → psychiatry or an ADHD specialist

Routes people commonly discuss with a PCP. Whether to refer is your clinician’s call.

Why this page exists

Fatigue, brain fog, joint pain and palpitations are shared symptoms: each appears in at least four of the ten conditions this page maps (fatigue in all ten), which is why pattern-matching on any one of them misroutes people. The fix isn’t more testing for everyone; it’s keeping your own record and putting the consider-list next to it, so the explanation that remains has actually earned it.

Everything stays on your device, saved in your browser only (Clear all removes it); the printouts, image, text copies and backup file are generated locally, and a passphrase-protected backup is encrypted in the browser before saving.
