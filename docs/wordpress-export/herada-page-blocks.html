Why it matters

The friction is predictable. The fallout doesn’t have to be.

Denials

Coverage gaps and prior-authorization issues surface late, after care is already in motion — when they’re hardest to fix.

Delays

Back-and-forth between patients, clinics, and insurers stalls treatment plans that were ready weeks earlier.

Surprises

Unexpected bills erode trust in care itself. Patients who can see costs coming stay in care longer.
